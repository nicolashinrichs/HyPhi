## 3. Reconciliation strategy

The core tension is structural: Felipe's work is a **clean 25-PR linear stack** (each PR = one focused commit, mergeable in order), while ours is **one large body** (18 committed review-round fixes + a big uncommitted homogenization pass) on `fix/coherence-pass`, never pushed. These are not rivals: his stack is mostly **new capability** (modality adapters, accel backend, embeddings, registry, packaging) and ours is mostly **correctness + hygiene** on the pre-existing core (the stats false-positive repair, config cwd safety, lint 279->0, NumPy docstrings, +180 tests). They are about 80% disjoint by file. The right move is to land both, his stack first as the new substrate, ours rebased on top, with a handful of surgical reconciliations.

Recommended order:

1. **Merge Felipe's stack first, in PR order**, treating it as the new substrate. The 13 pure new-feature PRs (#41, #45, #47, #51, #53, #54, #55, #56, #57, #58, #60, #62, and the subpackage half of #42) have zero conflict with our tree and bring real capability we deliberately deferred or never scoped. Several close open issues (#42->#25, #45->#28/#11, #55->#14, #51 exposes the flow).

2. **Take HIS on the overlap PRs where his is better and additive:** #44 (richer module docstrings), #46 (derangement warning + tests), #48 (PLV consolidation), #50 (the `sim_graph` rewrite — the one genuine code conflict; his single-`add_edge` is atomically correct and ours retains a subtler double-add), #52 (intra-brain nulls + the `.get()` guard our tree is missing).

3. **Keep OURS where ours supersedes:** #37 (lazy import + viz extra, with our noqa), #38 (stronger `test_hyphi.py` + xfail #28 + config tests), #40 (CI with import-sweep + frozen sync + `--extra develop`; borrow only his matrix label and the trigger-filter drop), #43 (broader NumPy docstring pass + the `gaps` sign constraint). Do not let his thinner versions overwrite these.

4. **COMBINE on the three partial-overlap PRs:** #42 (his subpackage `__init__.py` + our top-level `__all__` curation), #59 (his `viz/` package + our `_edge_curvature_for_plot` Ricci/Forman probe ported into `dyadic.py`, and preserve our `plt.colorbar(ax=...)` fix), #63 (his sympy dep + fmri extra + `release.yml` + resolved metadata; our mkdocs-material + tighter pins for the docs extra).

5. **Surface two decisions to Nico (do not auto-resolve):** (a) #61/#62/#63 all assume `main.py` exists, but we intentionally removed it for the library-first stance — decide whether to restore a thin `run_pipeline`/`__main__` entry (his `run_pipeline` is a harmless clean composition) or stay library-only and trim the README/`[project.scripts]` references. (b) #60's open question of whether hyperbolic embeddings replace, parallel, or validate the connectivity-to-graph step. Also confirm the methodological defaults flagged in #54 (fNIRS correlation vs coherence) and #55 (fMRI Pearson vs partial).

6. **Branch mechanics:** because our consolidation is unpushed and large, do not try to rebase it onto a moving 25-commit stack piecemeal. Either (a) let Felipe's stack merge to `master`, then rebase `fix/coherence-pass` once onto the new `master`, resolving the small set of reconciliations above (primarily `curvatures.py` #50, `modeling/__init__.py` #42/#51, `windowing.py`/`benchmarks.py` #48, `null_models.py`/`stats.py` #46/#52, `pyproject.toml` #63); or (b) split our pass into a few focused PRs (stats repair, config safety, lint+docstrings, tests) that slot into his stacked-PR workflow more cleanly. After reconciliation, run the union suite (his ~175 + our 315) and require green before merge. Net result: his breadth (modalities + accel + packaging) on top of our depth (a sound stats null, safe configs, 0 lint, NumPy docstrings, +180 tests) — complementary, not competing.
